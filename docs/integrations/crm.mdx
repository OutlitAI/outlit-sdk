Your CRM supplies the commercial spine: accounts, contacts, opportunities, owners — and when the Renewals responsibility is enabled, the renewal opportunities that become tracked renewal cycles.

## Connect

From **Integrations**, pick your CRM — **Salesforce**, **HubSpot**, or **Attio**. All three connect through OAuth: approve access, return to Outlit, and the initial import begins.

| CRM | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Salesforce** | Use your existing pipeline configuration; map its renewal stages below. |
| **HubSpot** | Map pipelines and deal stages after connecting. |
| **Attio** | Lists and objects map to accounts and opportunities. |

Connecting the CRM is an admin task. OAuth scopes are read-oriented — Outlit reads accounts, opportunities, and contacts; it does not write records back to your CRM.

## Map your renewal pipeline

After OAuth completes, the connect flow asks you to configure each pipeline:

- which **open stages** represent renewal deals,
- which closed stages count as **won** vs **lost** renewal outcomes,
- the **renewal date field** on the deal — the field Outlit reads as the cycle's date.

This mapping is what turns CRM opportunities into tracked renewal cycles with correct dates and outcomes. Skipping it — or mapping a field that holds no valid dates — leaves the CRM connected but renewals untracked, a common reason the Renewals portfolio is empty.

## What flows in

| Data | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Accounts | Customer profiles; merged with billing/product identities |
| Contacts | Customer → People tab; stakeholders in renewal briefs |
| Opportunities | Renewal cycles; prior-deal comparison on renewal detail |
| Owners | Customer ownership (drives who members see and who gets notified) |

## Renewal outcomes

When a renewal closes, the outcome can be linked to the CRM opportunity that closed it — the renewal detail keeps the outcome source distinct (CRM-linked vs manual vs billing-confirmed). Closed-stage mapping is what lets won/lost opportunities resolve cycles cleanly.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Renewals is empty after connecting">
    Check the CRM connection's pipeline mapping — renewal stages, won/lost outcomes, and the renewal-date field all need to be set. If they're set and still empty, the deals may be missing or carrying invalid values in the mapped date field.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CRM accounts don't match existing customers">
    Accounts match by domain and identifiers; ambiguous matches wait for confirmation rather than auto-merging. Check the customer's **Coverage** tab for pending matches or identity issues.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Ownership looks wrong">
    Ownership typically syncs from the CRM owner field. Fix it in your CRM, or adjust the customer owner directly in Outlit — local owner changes are supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Duplicate or missing deals">
    Stale or non-renewal opportunities can surface during initial mapping. Refine the stage mapping; renewal cycles can also be voided manually if a non-real cycle slipped in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
