Stripe is the billing source that defines which customers Outlit monitors — churn monitoring covers **paying** and **past-due** customers, and Stripe supplies MRR, subscription state, and billing-confirmed renewal outcomes.

## Connect

Outlit supports two Stripe connection methods. The setup form shows the method enabled for your workspace:

| Method | Best for | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **OAuth** (Stripe App) | Most workspaces | Redirect to Stripe, approve access, return to Outlit |
| **Restricted key** | Environments where the OAuth app isn't available | Paste a key starting with `rk_` |

Use a restricted key, not a secret key — it scopes Outlit to read access on customers, subscriptions, invoices, and the related objects it needs. Keep the key rotatable; if you revoke it in Stripe, the connection shows an error until you reconnect.

If you need another method, ask your Outlit contact.

## After connecting

1. **Historical sync runs in the background.** Customers, subscriptions, and invoices import; MRR appears on profiles as data lands.
2. **Finish webhook setup if prompted.** Credential connect and event webhooks can be separate steps — the connection's detail panel shows whether webhooks are live.
3. **Billing customers match to your accounts** — see [identity notes](#customers-that-dont-match) below if a Stripe customer lands on the wrong profile.

## What billing enables

- The monitored customer set (paying + past-due) for the Churn responsibility
- MRR and plan context on customer profiles and Attention cards
- Billing signals — delinquency, subscription changes — inside churn cases
- Billing-confirmed outcomes on renewal cycles

## Customers that don't match

Stripe customers are matched to Outlit customers by domain, email, and stored identifiers. Where a match is ambiguous, the link waits for confirmation rather than guessing — unmatched or mismatched billing customers surface on the customer's **Coverage** tab or as an identity issue. Confirming a match is a deliberate click; see [Customers](/product/customers).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Monitoring shows 'no billing source'">
    The Stripe connection errored or never finished — open Integrations and check its status. Reconnect, then check **Responsibilities → Churn** for current coverage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some customers show no MRR">
    The Stripe customer isn't matched to the Outlit customer yet — check the Coverage tab for a pending or suggested match.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Webhooks show inactive">
    Webhook setup is separate from credentials. Finish it from the Stripe connection detail, or ask your Outlit contact if the workspace was provisioned with guided setup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
