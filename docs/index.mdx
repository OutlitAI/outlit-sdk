## How Outlit works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Connect the systems that hold your customer context: CRM, billing, product usage, support, and
    conversations. The Outlit team helps you set up your workspace and monitoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your first attention items">
    Outlit connects records into customer profiles and surfaces the accounts that need
    attention now, with the evidence behind each finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the next step">
    An item may include a proposed approach. Choose how to proceed, and review any email draft
    before Outlit sends it. See [actions and approvals](/product/actions-and-approvals).
  </Step>
</Steps>
