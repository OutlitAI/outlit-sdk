Outlit continuously monitors your customers, finds churn risks and growth opportunities, and prepares the next step for your team to review — with the evidence behind every call.

It is built for the part of your book of business nobody can watch closely enough: the long tail of accounts that renew, expand, or quietly drift away while your team focuses on strategic names.

## How Outlit works

1. **Connect** — You link the systems that already hold your customer data: billing (Stripe), product usage (PostHog, Mixpanel, or Outlit SDKs), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio), conversations (Gmail, Slack, Granola, Gong, and more), and support (Pylon).
2. **Resolve** — Outlit reconciles people, accounts, domains, and billing records into customer profiles, keeping provenance on what they're built from.
3. **Monitor** — Outlit's responsibilities watch eligible customers continuously: Churn looks for retention risk, Renewals prepares upcoming renewals.
4. **Review** — Anything that needs a person lands in **Attention**: the risk, the evidence, and — where Outlit has one — a proposed approach.
5. **Approve** — You steer. Choosing an approach or approving an email draft is a human decision; Outlit executes only what you approve, like sending the reviewed Gmail follow-up.

## Getting access

Your Outlit team provisions the workspace during onboarding. After you receive an invitation, sign in to your company's workspace at [app.outlit.ai](https://app.outlit.ai). Then:
- A **workspace admin** can invite teammates from **Settings → Members**, or during onboarding.
- Each teammate connects their own work accounts where relevant (for example, Gmail connects per person, not once per company).

If you joined an existing workspace, [check its connected sources](/getting-started/connect-data). Connect your own Gmail if your team uses email as customer evidence.

## Where things live

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Attention** | Your queue: churn cases and renewal items that need a person, sorted by what's recommended |
| **Customers** | The accounts Outlit has resolved, with profiles, people, and source coverage |
| **Responsibilities** | What Outlit is doing for you: **Churn** monitoring status, cases, and watches; **Renewals** portfolio and briefs |
| **Integrations** | Connect and maintain your data sources |
| **Settings** | Members, Slack **Destinations**, **Website Visitors** tracking, **API keys**, **CLI & MCP**, billing |

The [Responsibilities guide](/product/responsibilities) explains monitoring status and workspace availability.
