Activation can be a definition, not just one event. `outlit_preview_activation` takes a `definition` (all or any of 1-5 conditions, each with a count or active days, a window, an optional property equality and, for the account, a number of people) and returns its reach and separation over new accounts. `outlit_get_settings` shows a saved definition, and `outlit_update_settings` takes an optional `activationDefinition`.
